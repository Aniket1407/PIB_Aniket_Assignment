### Question 1: What is RAG, and when would you use it?

**Answer:**

RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation. In simple words, it allows an AI model to first search for relevant information from a given source, like documents or a database, and then use that information to generate an answer.

I would use RAG when the AI needs to answer questions based on specific or updated information, such as company documents, policies, manuals, or a knowledge base.

### Question 2: What is the difference between AI, Machine Learning, and Generative AI?

**Answer:**

- **AI (Artificial Intelligence):** The broad concept of making computers perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.
- **Machine Learning (ML):** A part of AI where the system learns patterns from data instead of being explicitly programmed for every situation.
- **Generative AI:** A type of AI that can create new content such as text, images, code, audio, or videos based on the input it receives.

*Summary example:* AI is the overall field, ML is one way of making systems intelligent, and Generative AI focuses on creating new content.

### Question 3: What is a prompt in AI?

**Answer:**

A prompt is basically the instruction or question that we give to an AI model to get a particular response.

For example, if I ask an AI, *"Explain RAG in simple language with an example,"* that instruction is the prompt.

A clear and specific prompt usually helps the AI give a more useful response.

### Question 5: What is the difference between AI and a Chatbot?

**Answer:**

AI is a broad technology that can be used for many different tasks, such as image recognition, recommendations, prediction, automation, and content generation.

A chatbot is an application that uses AI to communicate with users through conversation.

For example, a chatbot can use AI to understand a user's question and provide an answer. So, a chatbot is one possible application of AI, not the same thing as AI itself.
